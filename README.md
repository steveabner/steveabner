## About Me

I am a Desktop Support Technician with hands-on experience troubleshooting Windows systems, supporting users, managing hardware, and resolving software and network-related issues.

My long-term goal is to transition into cybersecurity. I am currently building practical experience through security labs, technical projects, and continued training in SIEM analysis, vulnerability management, Windows administration, networking, and incident investigation.

## Objective

I am working toward a cybersecurity role where I can combine my IT support experience, troubleshooting skills, and growing security knowledge to help investigate alerts, identify threats, and protect systems and users.

I am especially interested in Security Operations, Blue Team operations, vulnerability management, and security engineering.
